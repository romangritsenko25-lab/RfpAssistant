# Data and AI engineering services

## Team and scope

The data and AI practice has 22 specialists: data engineers, machine learning engineers and analysts. The practice delivers data warehouses and lakes, streaming pipelines, forecasting models and assistants based on large language models (LLMs).

## LLM-based solutions

For retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) systems the team uses PostgreSQL with pgvector or Qdrant as the vector store. Answers must be grounded in retrieved documents and carry a reference to the source. Both hosted models and locally run open-weight models are supported; for customers who cannot send data outside their perimeter, the model runs on the customer's own infrastructure.

## MLOps

Models are versioned in MLflow. Training and inference pipelines are executed in CI/CD, every model release is accompanied by a quality report with metrics, data version and date of evaluation. Drift monitoring is configured for models in production and reviewed monthly.

## Data protection in AI projects

Personal data is not sent to third-party model providers without the written consent of the customer. Training data used for customer models is stored in the customer environment and deleted within 30 days after the project ends unless the contract states otherwise.
