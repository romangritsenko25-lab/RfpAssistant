# Supplier and subcontractor security policy

## Risk tiers

Every supplier is assigned to one of three risk tiers before the contract is signed. Tier 1 covers suppliers that process customer data or have access to production; Tier 2 covers suppliers with access to internal systems only; Tier 3 covers all others.

## Due diligence

Tier 1 suppliers complete a security questionnaire and provide evidence of controls, such as a valid ISO/IEC 27001 certificate. A data processing agreement and a non-disclosure agreement are mandatory for Tier 1 and Tier 2. The right to audit is included in the contracts with Tier 1 suppliers.

## Ongoing monitoring

Tier 1 suppliers are reviewed every year; Tier 2 suppliers every two years. Security incidents on the supplier side must be reported to the company within 24 hours after the supplier becomes aware of them.

## Subprocessors

Customers whose data is processed by a subprocessor are informed in writing at least 30 days before a subprocessor is added or replaced. A customer may object within this period. The current list of subprocessors is provided to a customer on request.
