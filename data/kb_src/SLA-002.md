# SLA tiers matrix

## Service tiers

| Parameter | Standard | Premium | Enterprise |
|---|---|---|---|
| Monthly availability | 99.5% | 99.9% | 99.95% |
| Support hours | Mon-Fri 9:00-18:00 (UTC+5) | 24x7 for P1 and P2 | 24x7 for P1 and P2 |
| P1 resolution target | 8 hours | 4 hours | 2 hours |
| P2 resolution target | 2 business days | 1 business day | 8 hours |
| Named service manager | No | No | Yes |
| Quarterly service review | No | Yes | Yes |
| Planned maintenance, max per month | 8 hours | 8 hours | 4 hours |

## Reporting

| Report | Frequency | Delivered |
|---|---|---|
| Availability and incident report | Monthly | By the 5th business day of the next month |
| Security and vulnerability summary | Quarterly | Premium and Enterprise only |
| Service review meeting | Quarterly | Premium and Enterprise only |

## Notes

The Enterprise tier was introduced on 1 June 2025. Planned maintenance requires notice of at least 5 days.
