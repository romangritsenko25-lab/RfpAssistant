# ISO/IEC 27001 certification status

## Certification

Steppelight Technologies holds an ISO/IEC 27001:2022 certificate issued on 12 November 2024 and valid until 11 November 2027. The scope covers software development, cloud operations and technical support for customers. The certificate was issued by a fictional certification body, KazCertAudit.

## Surveillance audit 2025

The annual surveillance audit took place in October 2025. It resulted in no major nonconformities and two minor ones. Both minor nonconformities (incomplete evidence of a quarterly access review and a late update of the asset register) were closed on 28 November 2025.

## Statement of Applicability

The current version of the Statement of Applicability, 4.2, includes 93 controls of Annex A, of which 3 are marked as not applicable (physical delivery of media, broadcast systems and a custom hardware development). The statement is available to customers under a non-disclosure agreement.

## Internal audits

Internal audits of the information security management system are performed twice a year by trained auditors who are independent from the audited units. The management review is held once a year.
