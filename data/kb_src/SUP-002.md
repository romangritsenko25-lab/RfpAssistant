# Support escalation matrix and response targets

## Response targets

The following initial response targets apply from 10 February 2026. For priority P1 the first response is given within 15 minutes; for P2 within 1 hour; for P3 within 4 business hours; for P4 within 1 business day. For Premium and Enterprise customers the P1 and P2 targets apply 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

## Escalation path for P1

If no progress is made on a P1 incident within 30 minutes, it is escalated from the first line to the second line engineers. After 1 hour without a workaround, the head of delivery joins the incident. After 2 hours, the chief technology officer (CTO) is informed and takes ownership of customer communication.

## Customer communication

During a P1 incident the customer receives status updates every 30 minutes until a workaround is in place, and every 2 hours afterwards. A written incident report is sent within 5 business days after resolution.

## Contacts

The customer-facing escalation contacts are listed in the support contact sheet attached to each contract. Contact data are reviewed every quarter.
