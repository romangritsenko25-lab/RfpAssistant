# Release and change management procedure

## Change classes

Changes to production are divided into three classes. Standard changes are pre-approved and low risk. Normal changes require approval of the Change Advisory Board (CAB). Emergency changes are made to restore service or close a critical vulnerability and are reviewed afterwards.

## Change Advisory Board

The CAB meets weekly on Tuesday at 11:00 Astana time (UTC+5). The board consists of the head of delivery, the head of operations, the information security lead and the product owner of the affected system.

## Release windows

Planned production releases are performed from Tuesday to Thursday between 22:00 and 02:00 Astana time. Releases are not made on Fridays, weekends or public holidays in Kazakhstan, except emergency changes. Customers are notified about planned maintenance at least 5 days in advance.

## Records

Each change has a record with a description, risk assessment, rollback plan and test evidence. Change records are retained for 3 years. After an emergency change a review is held within 3 business days.
